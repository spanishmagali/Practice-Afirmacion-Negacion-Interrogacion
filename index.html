Essential Grammar Rule

Los Opuestos (Opposites)

In Spanish, many affirmative words have direct negative opposites. Remember the "Double Negative" rule: If the negative word follows the verb, you must put "no" before the verb. Example: No veo a nadie. (I don't see anyone).

Affirmative (Afirmativo)
Alguien (someone)
Algo (something)
Siempre (always)
También (also/too)
Negative (Negativo)
Nadie (no one)
Nada (nothing)
Nunca / Jamás (never)
Tampoco (neither/not either)
